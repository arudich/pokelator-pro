Estimated full-HP 1v1s · usage-weighted builds and items

Each opponent contributes its win rate (0–1) to the total score. Meta Mode (on by default) weights stat-distribution scoring by measured Smogon Champions usage; off weights opponents equally. Weights are normalized over the displayed opponents, with paired fields sharing one weight. The source is the monthly Showdown ladder, not the in-game ladder. The optimizer reallocates all 66 SP across all six stats with a cap of 32 per stat, holding nature, moves, ability and item fixed. This approximate multi-start search uses only the current Top N list. It checks physical/special bulk alternatives, screens at 16 trials per matchup, then follows local improvements at 64 trials, with a combined limit of 160 spread evaluations. Promising spreads are validated at 256 trials. Sampled opponent builds, earlier trials and repeated damage calculations are reused. Shared seeds reduce comparison noise; the displayed best score is always validated at 256 trials and never falls below the original build. This remains an in-sample estimate, not an exhaustive or independently validated optimum. Preview does not edit the saved team.

We Win is 85–100% estimated wins; We Lose is 0–15%. The middle tiers split at 50%; exact ties are Close But Unfavored. Sprite badges, tier placement and sorting all use simulated wins across all trials, including cases with unresolved sets. A dash means no modeled result. Hover retains conditional rates, modeled coverage, turn order and item details. Estimates use 256 seeded trials per opponent/field scenario.

Our saved build and moves are exact; both sides start at full HP with their selected stat stages. Opponents sample reported stat spreads, full natures, abilities and common items, including Speed items. Mega Stones are required. Missing item/nature probability uses no item/neutral nature; only reported spreads are modeled. The two most common moves are always present; two further moves are sampled by relative usage without replacement. Coverage weights are conditional, not the original inclusion percentages. These statistics are separate distributions, not observed joint builds.

Fixed-core model cautions

    This is a direct-attack duel estimate, not a complete battle AI: setup, recovery/status-move strategies, switching, recharge/charge attacks and contextual counter/OHKO moves are excluded. Best direct attacks, Speed/priority, accuracy, damage rolls, multi-hit attacks, offensive self-drops, recoil, common passive healing/berries, Focus Sash/Sturdy/Disguise and selected field effects are modeled. Attack selection uses a short KO-time heuristic. Duels stop after 20 turns; draws/unmodeled sets are reported. Critical hits, PP exhaustion and complex move/ability effects are not modeled; field conditions are held fixed. One active foe means no spread-damage penalty even when usage format is Doubles.
