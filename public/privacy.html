Your teams stay in your browser.

Pokelator saves teams, preferences and remembered builds in your browser. Anonymous usage counters, when enabled, count page loads, calculated matchups, teams created or imported, Pokémon added, and color edits or saves. Only daily action totals are stored; these counters do not send team contents, names, search text, visitor identifiers or cookies.

These are approximate action counts, not unique visitors, real battles played, or a count of currently saved teams. Browser blocking, batched delivery and public endpoint abuse can affect totals. Automated health-check browsers are excluded. Do Not Track and Global Privacy Control disable these counters.

Cloudflare hosts the site and receives normal network request information, including IP addresses, to deliver it. Pokémon artwork is loaded from championsbattledata.com. Aggregate usage is included in the maintainer’s health reports and may appear in public repository workflow artifacts.
